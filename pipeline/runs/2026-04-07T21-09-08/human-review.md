- [ ] Approve

- **Category:** privacy
- **Relevance:** high
- **Jurisdiction:** IL
- **Type:** court-decision
- **Summary:** On April 1, 2026, the U.S. Court of Appeals for the Seventh Circuit held that the August 2024 amendment to Illinois' Biometric Information Privacy Act (BIPA) applies retroactively to cases that were already pending when the amendment was enacted. The amendment limits BIPA damages so that repeated collection of the same biometric data using the same method counts as a single violation rather than a per-scan violation, capping recovery at $5,000 (intentional) or $1,000 (negligent) per person rather than per scan. The ruling reverses three federal district court decisions and significantly reduces potential liability exposure for employers and technology companies facing existing BIPA litigation.
