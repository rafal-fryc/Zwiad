# Rhode Island Enacts AI Healthcare Notification and Mental Health Oversight Laws

**Jurisdiction:** Rhode Island | **Category:** AI Law — Health | **Date:** 2026-07-23

## Summary [MEDIUM confidence]

On June 22, 2026, Governor Daniel McKee signed a package of Rhode Island AI-in-healthcare bills, most notably the **Use of Artificial Intelligence by Healthcare Providers Notification Act** (S 2570 / H 7538), which requires licensed healthcare providers and facilities to disclose to patients when AI tools (such as "ambient AI scribes") are used to document in-person or telehealth visits, and to give patients the ability to decline the use of such tools [Nixon Peabody](https://www.nixonpeabody.com/insights/alerts/2026/07/20/rhode-island-joins-the-wave-of-states-regulating-ai-in-healthcare-and-ai-companion-tools); [Healthcare IT News](https://www.healthcareitnews.com/news/rhode-island-passes-ambient-ai-scribe-opt-out-law); [Becker's Hospital Review](https://www.beckershospitalreview.com/healthcare-information-technology/ai/rhode-island-passes-ai-scribe-disclosure-bill-for-patient-visits/). The same signing package included the **Oversight of Artificial Intelligence Technology in Mental Health Care Act** (S 2197 / H 7349), which restricts AI's role in therapeutic decision-making, and a separate **AI Companion/Chatbot Safety Act** (S 2195/H 7350) addressing self-harm detection and crisis routing for companion chatbots — the latter is effective January 1, 2027 and is treated as a distinct, already-tracked topic in Zwiad's chatbot-law comparative report (see Related Reports) [Nixon Peabody](https://www.nixonpeabody.com/insights/alerts/2026/07/20/rhode-island-joins-the-wave-of-states-regulating-ai-in-healthcare-and-ai-companion-tools). This report focuses on the healthcare-provider notification act and the mental-health AI oversight act, both effective June 22, 2026, since those are the laws described in the underlying Lexology/Norton Rose Fulbright finding as "AI use in healthcare and related patient privacy protections."

## Key Facts [MEDIUM confidence]

- The Notification Act (S 2570, lead-sponsored by Sen. Pamela Lauria, and companion H 7538, lead-sponsored by Rep. Teresa Tanzi) requires "healthcare providers" — defined broadly to include physicians, physician assistants, dentists, registered and licensed practical nurses, advanced practice registered nurses, nursing assistants, and other state-licensed professionals — and healthcare facilities that use AI to document patient visits to notify patients of that use and to review AI-generated documentation for accuracy after the visit [Healthcare IT News](https://www.healthcareitnews.com/news/rhode-island-passes-ambient-ai-scribe-opt-out-law); [Becker's Hospital Review](https://www.beckershospitalreview.com/healthcare-information-technology/ai/rhode-island-passes-ai-scribe-disclosure-bill-for-patient-visits/).
- Patients must be given the ability to opt out of AI-assisted documentation ("ambient AI scribes") if they are uncomfortable with it, according to sponsor statements and press coverage [independentri.com](https://www.independentri.com/news/article_40d51c76-2c06-4816-99c0-66f57b90e834.html); [Healthcare IT News](https://www.healthcareitnews.com/news/rhode-island-passes-ambient-ai-scribe-opt-out-law).
- The Senate passed S 2570 on June 10, 2026 by a 37-0 vote, and Governor McKee signed the bill (alongside H 7538 and the two other AI-healthcare bills discussed below) on June 22, 2026 [Nixon Peabody](https://www.nixonpeabody.com/insights/alerts/2026/07/20/rhode-island-joins-the-wave-of-states-regulating-ai-in-healthcare-and-ai-companion-tools); [Transparency Coalition](https://www.transparencycoalition.ai/news/rhode-island-enacts-four-new-ai-laws-including-a-therapy-chatbot-ban).
- Companion measure S 2197/H 7349, the Oversight of Artificial Intelligence Technology in Mental Health Care Act, bars AI from independently practicing therapy or being used by clinicians to interpret patient emotions or create treatment plans; mental health professionals retain the ability to use AI for administrative or supplementary tasks, but cannot outsource therapeutic decision-making to it. This act is also effective June 22, 2026 [Nixon Peabody](https://www.nixonpeabody.com/insights/alerts/2026/07/20/rhode-island-joins-the-wave-of-states-regulating-ai-in-healthcare-and-ai-companion-tools); [Transparency Coalition](https://www.transparencycoalition.ai/news/rhode-island-enacts-four-new-ai-laws-including-a-therapy-chatbot-ban).
- The Rhode Island ACLU and the Senior Agenda Coalition of Rhode Island supported the Notification Act in committee [independentri.com](https://www.independentri.com/news/article_40d51c76-2c06-4816-99c0-66f57b90e834.html).
- Coverage is not fully consistent on specifics: one secondary source states the Notification Act took effect June 16, 2026, while the more detailed Nixon Peabody client alert and multiple other outlets state the effective date is June 22, 2026 (the signing date) — this report treats June 22, 2026 as the effective date, corroborated by the greater number of independent sources, but flags the discrepancy for confirmation against the enacted statute text [Data Protection Report](https://www.dataprotectionreport.com/2026/07/rhode-islands-new-ai-and-healthcare-privacy-law/); [Nixon Peabody](https://www.nixonpeabody.com/insights/alerts/2026/07/20/rhode-island-joins-the-wave-of-states-regulating-ai-in-healthcare-and-ai-companion-tools).

## Action Items

- Hospitals, physician practices, and telehealth providers operating in Rhode Island that use ambient AI scribes or other AI documentation tools should implement patient-facing notice and opt-out workflows before further reliance on the June 22, 2026 effective date; confirm the exact statutory language and any penalty provisions once the enacted S 2570A/H 7538A text is retrieved.
- Behavioral health and mental health provider organizations using AI tools for clinical documentation, emotion interpretation, or treatment planning should audit current AI use to ensure clinicians retain sole responsibility for therapeutic decision-making, consistent with S 2197/H 7349.
- Compliance and privacy teams should track the separate AI Companion/Chatbot Safety Act (S 2195/H 7350), effective January 1, 2027, as a distinct compliance deadline — see the Related Reports section for Zwiad's existing coverage of Rhode Island's companion-chatbot law within the broader multi-state chatbot comparative report.
- Confirm the Notification Act's effective date (June 16 vs. June 22, 2026) and any Rhode Island Public Law chapter number directly against the Rhode Island General Assembly / Secretary of State's records, since secondary sources disagree.
