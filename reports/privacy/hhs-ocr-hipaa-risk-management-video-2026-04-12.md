# HHS OCR Releases Risk Management Guidance Video Under HIPAA Security Rule

## Executive Summary [HIGH confidence]

In early April 2026, the U.S. Department of Health and Human Services (HHS) Office for Civil Rights (OCR) released a video presentation titled "The HIPAA Security Rule: Risk Management," featuring Nicholas Heesters, OCR's Senior Advisor for Cybersecurity. The video explains the Risk Management standard at 45 C.F.R. § 164.308(a)(1)(ii)(B), provides examples of risk management failures identified in OCR breach investigations, and answers a selection of questions OCR solicited from regulated entities in December 2025. The release accompanies a public statement from OCR Director Paula M. Stannard that the agency's 2026 enforcement initiative will extend beyond risk analysis to target noncompliance with the risk management requirement. Coverage of the release was reported by [HIPAA Journal](https://www.hipaajournal.com/ocr-risk-management-guidance-video/) and [DataBreaches.net](https://databreaches.net/2026/04/08/ocr-releases-risk-management-video/).

## Background: The Risk Management Standard [HIGH confidence]

The HIPAA Security Rule requires every covered entity and business associate to "[i]mplement security measures sufficient to reduce risks and vulnerabilities to a reasonable and appropriate level" to comply with the general security standards at 45 C.F.R. § 164.306(a). This Risk Management implementation specification sits alongside the Risk Analysis requirement at § 164.308(a)(1)(ii)(A) in the Security Management Process standard. While OCR has published guidance on Risk Analysis for more than a decade — including the long-standing [Guidance on Risk Analysis Requirements under the HIPAA Security Rule](https://www.hhs.gov/sites/default/files/ocr/privacy/hipaa/administrative/securityrule/rafinalguidancepdf.pdf) — the Risk Management counterpart has received comparatively less dedicated attention in OCR's publicly released educational materials.

The full catalog of OCR Security Rule guidance is maintained at the [Security Rule Guidance Material](https://www.hhs.gov/hipaa/for-professionals/security/guidance/index.html) page on HHS.gov.

## The April 2026 Video Release [HIGH confidence]

According to [HIPAA Journal's coverage](https://www.hipaajournal.com/ocr-risk-management-guidance-video/), the video is hosted on OCR's YouTube channel (@USGovHHSOCR) and addresses three principal topics:

1. **HIPAA Security Rule risk management requirements** — what the standard obliges covered entities and business associates to do once risks and vulnerabilities have been identified in a risk analysis.
2. **Risk management and cybersecurity resources** — practical tools and frameworks (including NIST guidance) that regulated entities can use to satisfy the standard.
3. **OCR investigations that have identified potential risk management violations** — examples drawn from recent breach investigations and resolution agreements.

The final segment of the presentation answers questions OCR solicited from the regulated community in December 2025. [DataBreaches.net](https://databreaches.net/2026/04/08/ocr-releases-risk-management-video/) confirmed the release on April 8, 2026.

## Enforcement Context [HIGH confidence]

OCR Director Paula M. Stannard publicly confirmed that the agency's ongoing Risk Analysis Enforcement Initiative — launched in late 2024 — will "continue and evolve" in 2026 to also target noncompliance with the Risk Management requirement, as summarized by [Accountable HQ's analysis of 2026 enforcement priorities](https://www.accountablehq.com/post/hhs-ocr-enforcement-priorities-for-2026-hipaa-risk-analysis-in-focus). The expansion is significant because OCR's historic enforcement settlements have repeatedly cited entities for failing to translate identified risks into concrete mitigation measures; the new guidance signals that OCR intends to hold entities accountable not only for performing an analysis but for acting on its findings.

## Relationship to the Pending Security Rule NPRM [MEDIUM confidence]

The video release comes against the backdrop of the [HIPAA Security Rule Notice of Proposed Rulemaking](https://www.hhs.gov/hipaa/for-professionals/security/hipaa-security-rule-nprm/factsheet/index.html) (published December 2024), which would substantially strengthen the Security Rule's risk analysis and risk management obligations. Under the NPRM, risk assessments would be required to include a written technology asset inventory and network map, identification of all reasonably anticipated threats and vulnerabilities, and assigned risk levels — conducted at least every 12 months. While the NPRM has not yet been finalized, OCR's April 2026 video can be read as reinforcing expectations that will only become more stringent if the rule is adopted.

## Action Items for Regulated Entities

- **Review the video** and circulate it among security officers, privacy officers, and the workforce members responsible for the Security Management Process.
- **Audit risk management documentation** to confirm that each risk identified in the most recent risk analysis has a documented, implemented, or formally accepted mitigation, and that residual risk is tracked over time.
- **Align with NIST frameworks**, particularly NIST SP 800-30 (risk assessment) and the NIST Cybersecurity Framework, both of which OCR has referenced in guidance as acceptable methodologies.
- **Prepare for OCR audits** that request evidence of ongoing risk management — not just a single point-in-time risk analysis — including change-management records, patch logs, and board- or committee-level risk acceptance decisions.
- **Monitor the Security Rule NPRM** for a final rule, which would codify many of the documentation practices OCR now emphasizes informally.
