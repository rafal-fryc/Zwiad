# New Mexico Seeks $3.7 Billion and Platform Overhaul from Meta in Children's Online Safety Trial Phase 2

**Jurisdiction:** New Mexico | **Category:** Privacy | **Date:** May 5, 2026

## Summary [HIGH confidence]

New Mexico Attorney General Raúl Torrez is seeking $3.7 billion over 15 years and sweeping changes to Meta's platforms in the second phase of a landmark trial before First Judicial District Court Judge Bryan Biedscheid. The state is asking the court to declare Meta a public nuisance and fund a comprehensive abatement plan to address harms to children caused by Facebook and Instagram. The case follows a [March 2026 jury verdict](https://nmdoj.gov/press-release/new-mexico-department-of-justice-wins-landmark-verdict-against-meta/) ordering Meta to pay $375 million in civil penalties — a verdict that stands as the first trial win in more than 40 state AG lawsuits over Meta's child safety practices.

## Key Facts [HIGH confidence]

- **Phase 1 verdict (March 24, 2026):** A Santa Fe jury found Meta willfully violated New Mexico's [Unfair Practices Act, NMSA 1978 § 57-12-1 et seq.](https://law.justia.com/codes/new-mexico/chapter-57/article-12/), ordering $375 million at the statutory maximum of $5,000 per violation; Meta has vowed to appeal ([NMDOJ press release](https://nmdoj.gov/press-release/new-mexico-department-of-justice-wins-landmark-verdict-against-meta/)).
- **Phase 2 is a bench trial:** The second phase opened May 4, 2026, before Judge Biedscheid without a jury, and is scheduled to run three weeks; it focuses on whether Meta's platforms constitute a public nuisance and what abatement is required ([CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/05/04/meta-new-mexico-child-safety-facebook-instagram.html)).
- **$3.7 billion, 15-year abatement plan:** Prosecutors are seeking funds for public education programs, mental health providers, schools, and law enforcement, in addition to structural changes to Meta's services ([Claims Journal](https://www.claimsjournal.com/news/national/2026/05/05/337354.htm)).
- **Platform change demands:** The state seeks mandatory age verification, redesigned recommendation algorithms that prioritize child well-being over engagement metrics, elimination of infinite scroll and autoplay for minors, removal of "likes" tallies defaults, restrictions on end-to-end encryption that hinder CSAM reporting, mandatory parent/guardian linkage for minor accounts, and a court-supervised child safety monitor ([NBC San Diego](https://www.nbcsandiego.com/news/national-international/new-mexico-meta-apps-algorithms-child-safety-trial/4019207/)).
- **Judge signals concern about overreach:** Before opening statements, Judge Biedscheid said "I am a judge, I am not a legislator, I am not a regulator," warning he was concerned some demanded changes could amount to overreach and cautioning against turning the court into a "one-person legislature" ([Source New Mexico](https://sourcenm.com/2026/05/04/judge-warns-new-mexico-prosecutors-he-wont-overreach-as-bench-trial-against-meta-begins/)).
- **Meta's position:** Meta accuses the state of overreach, has threatened to eliminate service in New Mexico entirely if forced to comply with billion-dollar remedies, and has already announced its intent to appeal the Phase 1 verdict ([Santa Fe New Mexican](https://www.santafenewmexican.com/news/local_news/meta-accuses-new-mexico-of-overreach-cite-judges-concern-as-bench-trial-against-meta-begins/article_6131eeb3-0dfa-4363-8fef-7fce194a5673.html)).
- **Broader context:** New Mexico's case is the first of 40+ state AG lawsuits against Meta to reach trial; 34 other states pursue parallel federal claims in Multi-District Litigation 3047, while at least nine states including Massachusetts are litigating in state courts ([CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/05/04/meta-new-mexico-child-safety-facebook-instagram.html)).
- **Original complaint:** Filed December 5, 2023, as *State of New Mexico v. Meta Platforms, Inc.*, Case No. D-101-CV-2023-02838, First Judicial District Court, County of Santa Fe ([Complaint PDF — NMAG](https://nmag.gov/wp-content/uploads/2024/01/2023-12-05-NM-v.-Meta-et-al.-COMPLAINT-REDACTED.pdf)).

## Action Items

- **Monitor Phase 2 outcome:** Judge Biedscheid's ruling on public nuisance and abatement will set a precedent for what remedies state courts may impose on platforms. Track the docket in Case No. D-101-CV-2023-02838 for a ruling, which is expected within weeks of the three-week bench trial closing.
- **Assess age verification obligations now:** Even if the New Mexico court narrows the remedies, age verification is increasingly a baseline expectation across state AG enforcement. Platforms serving minors should audit their current verification mechanisms against emerging state-level standards.
- **Review recommendation algorithm governance:** The specific demand to redesign algorithms away from engagement optimization toward child well-being signals a regulatory direction that will extend well beyond New Mexico. Operators should document existing safeguards and evaluate gaps.
- **Watch Meta's appeal of Phase 1 verdict:** The $375 million Unfair Practices Act judgment is likely to be appealed in New Mexico courts. A ruling on the scope of state consumer-protection statutes as applied to platform design decisions will be significant for all major digital platforms operating in the state.
- **Track multistate AG coordination:** With more than 40 state AGs having filed parallel suits, a New Mexico win on public nuisance could accelerate similar Phase 2-style remedial proceedings in other states. Compliance officers should assess exposure in each state where AG actions are pending.
