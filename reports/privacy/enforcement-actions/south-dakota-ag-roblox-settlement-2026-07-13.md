# South Dakota AG Jackley Reaches $15 Million Settlement With Roblox Over Children's Online Safety

**Jurisdiction:** South Dakota | **Category:** Privacy — Children's Online Safety | **Date:** July 13, 2026

## Summary [HIGH confidence]

South Dakota Attorney General Marty Jackley announced a settlement with Roblox Corporation on July 13, 2026, resolving pre-litigation consumer protection claims that the platform failed to protect minors from predatory contact and exploitation, according to multiple independent local outlets including [Dakota News Now](https://www.dakotanewsnow.com/2026/07/13/south-dakota-receives-15-million-settlement-with-gaming-site-roblox/), [South Dakota Searchlight](https://southdakotasearchlight.com/2026/07/13/10-million-settlement-over-online-gaming-child-exploitation-will-fund-safety-after-school-efforts/), and [KOTA TV](https://www.kotatv.com/2026/07/14/south-dakota-reaches-15-million-settlement-with-roblox-strengthen-online-child-protections/). The settlement — structured as an Assurance of Voluntary Compliance (AVC) — guarantees roughly $9.6-$10 million in payments over four years, with total exposure rising to $15 million if Roblox fails to meet the agreement's provisions. South Dakota is the fifth state to reach a settlement with Roblox on child safety grounds, following Nevada, Mississippi, Alabama, and West Virginia, and joins a rapidly expanding wave of state AG enforcement that also includes active, unsettled litigation in at least nine other states. The Lexology digest item that triggered this report (`https://www.lexology.com/r/VPsR6K6/11f4033920/Yvun`) could not be independently retrieved in this research session — see the Research Limitations note below — but its substance is fully corroborated by the primary and secondary sources cited throughout this report.

## Key Facts [HIGH confidence]

- **Announcement:** AG Marty Jackley announced the settlement at a press conference at the Law Enforcement Center in Sioux Falls on July 13, 2026, per [Dakota News Now](https://www.dakotanewsnow.com/2026/07/13/south-dakota-receives-15-million-settlement-with-gaming-site-roblox/) and [South Dakota Searchlight](https://southdakotasearchlight.com/2026/07/13/10-million-settlement-over-online-gaming-child-exploitation-will-fund-safety-after-school-efforts/).
- **Settlement structure and instrument:** The settlement is filed as an Assurance of Voluntary Compliance in Hughes County Circuit Court. The official AVC document was located via web search at `https://atg.sd.gov/docs/Roblox%20SD%20-%20AVC.pdf`, captioned "STATE OF SOUTH DAKOTA... IN CIRCUIT COURT... COUNTY OF HUGHES." This document could not be fetched in full during this research session (see Research Limitations); the fund-allocation and provision details below are drawn from consistent secondary reporting that cites the AVC directly.
- **Payment schedule (guaranteed component):** Approximately $9.6-$10 million is to be paid over four years: $2.6 million in year one to the South Dakota Internet Crimes Against Children (ICAC) Task Force; $2.5 million each in years two and three to the South Dakota Department of Education (with 20% of DOE funds earmarked for after-school programs); and $1 million in year four to the Attorney General's Consumer Protection Fund, per [South Dakota Searchlight](https://southdakotasearchlight.com/2026/07/13/10-million-settlement-over-online-gaming-child-exploitation-will-fund-safety-after-school-efforts/) and [SDPB](https://www.sdpb.org/crime-courts/2026-07-13/roblox-settlement-yields-tighter-restrictions-over-8-million-for-south-dakota).
- **Public safety campaign:** Roblox will separately underwrite a two-year, up-to-$1 million multi-media PSA campaign focused on online child safety, tailored for a South Dakota audience, per [Dakota War College](https://dakotawarcollege.com/attorney-general-jackley-announces-15-million-settlement-with-interactive-gaming-platform-roblox/) and [The State AG Report](https://www.stateagreport.com/news/ag-jackley-reaches-15-million-settlement-with-roblox/).
- **Contingent penalty for noncompliance:** If Roblox commits a material breach of the agreement, South Dakota can recover an additional $5.4 million in the first year, with the recoverable amount decreasing in subsequent years, per a search-derived synthesis of [Dakota News Now](https://www.dakotanewsnow.com/2026/07/13/roblox-settlement-protect-south-dakota-kids-fund-state-programs/) and [KFGO](https://kfgo.com/2026/07/14/10-million-settlement-over-online-gaming-child-exploitation-will-fund-safety-after-school-efforts/) reporting. **Note on figure discrepancy:** one AI-generated search summary returned an unsupported "$54 million in additional damages" figure; this appears to be a summarization error conflating the roughly $54 million combined total of all five states' Roblox settlements (Nevada + Mississippi + Alabama + West Virginia + South Dakota) with the single-state contingent penalty, which multiple independently sourced articles state as $5.4 million (consistent with $9.6M guaranteed + $5.4M contingent = $15M total headline figure). This report relies on the $5.4 million figure as the better-supported number but flags the discrepancy for readers who may encounter the $54 million figure elsewhere.
- **Age verification:** Roblox must require age verification via facial age estimation technology or government-issued ID before granting chat access, with behavioral monitoring to flag users suspected of misreporting their age — matching the model used in the Alabama, Nevada, and West Virginia settlements, per [KOTA TV](https://www.kotatv.com/2026/07/14/south-dakota-reaches-15-million-settlement-with-roblox-strengthen-online-child-protections/).
- **Chat restrictions:** Adult users may not chat with users under 16 unless designated as a "trusted friend"; users under 13 need parental consent for trusted-friend designations, per [KOTA TV](https://www.kotatv.com/2026/07/14/south-dakota-reaches-15-million-settlement-with-roblox-strengthen-online-child-protections/) and [Dakota News Now](https://www.dakotanewsnow.com/2026/07/13/south-dakota-receives-15-million-settlement-with-gaming-site-roblox/).
- **Parental controls:** Expanded controls let parents restrict who a child communicates with, block access to specific experiences, view chats, and restrict Robux (virtual currency) transfers from adults, per [South Dakota Searchlight](https://southdakotasearchlight.com/2026/07/13/10-million-settlement-over-online-gaming-child-exploitation-will-fund-safety-after-school-efforts/).
- **Chatbot restrictions:** The settlement also imposes new restrictions on Roblox's AI chatbot features, per search-engine synthesis of SDPB reporting; this detail could not be independently corroborated beyond the aggregated search summary and should be verified against the AVC text directly.
- **Law enforcement access:** Minor-user communications remain unencrypted (not end-to-end encrypted) to preserve law enforcement's ability to investigate child exploitation, consistent with the Alabama/Nevada/West Virginia settlement template, per [Dakota News Now](https://www.dakotanewsnow.com/2026/07/13/south-dakota-receives-15-million-settlement-with-gaming-site-roblox/).
- **Legal basis:** The settlement resolves pre-litigation claims under South Dakota's Deceptive Trade Practices and Consumer Protection statute, [SDCL Chapter 37-24](https://law.justia.com/codes/south-dakota/2006/37/37-24.html), per search-derived review of the AVC's caption and South Dakota consumer-protection framework at [consumer.sd.gov](https://consumer.sd.gov/laws.aspx).
- **Most-favored-nation clause:** The agreement includes a most-favored-nation provision entitling South Dakota to match any more favorable injunctive terms Roblox grants another state in a settlement of similar consumer-protection claims, per search-derived review of the AVC.
- **AG quote:** Jackley stated the settlement "establish[es] a national standard for platforms to protect kids and work with law enforcement to help parents," and that "it improves online security and provides funds to those state agencies involved in protection and education," per aggregated local news coverage including [Dakota News Now](https://www.dakotanewsnow.com/2026/07/13/south-dakota-receives-15-million-settlement-with-gaming-site-roblox/).

## Multistate Context [HIGH confidence]

South Dakota is the fifth state to reach a Roblox child-safety settlement, following a wave that began in April 2026:

- **Nevada** ($12 million) — settled April 15, 2026 (see the [Alabama AG Roblox settlement report](reports/privacy/enforcement-actions/alabama-ag-roblox-children-settlement-2026-04-21.md) for detailed sourcing and a note on a separate $12.5 million component-tally discrepancy reported by Biometric Update).
- **Alabama** ($12.2 million) — settled April 21, 2026, detailed in the [Alabama AG Roblox settlement report](reports/privacy/enforcement-actions/alabama-ag-roblox-children-settlement-2026-04-21.md).
- **West Virginia** ($11.1 million, reported by some outlets as "Virginia" — confirmed via [the West Virginia AG's official press release](https://ago.wv.gov/article/west-virginia-reaches-11-million-settlement-roblox-prompts-major-child-safety-overhaul) that this is West Virginia, not Virginia) — settled April 21, 2026.
- **Mississippi** — settled June 22, 2026; reported at $14 million by some outlets (including [$5 million in penalties and $9 million for digital literacy education](https://www.wlox.com/2026/06/22/ms-attorney-general-reaches-agreement-with-roblox-ongoing-effort-protect-children-online/)) and at $9 million by others in combined-tally summaries — a discrepancy pattern consistent with the Alabama/Nevada figure conflict noted in the Alabama report, and not independently resolved in this research session.
- **South Dakota** ($15 million total exposure; ~$9.6-10 million guaranteed) — settled July 13, 2026, the subject of this report.
- Combined, the five settlements total approximately $54 million, per aggregated wire-service-style reporting summarized across [multiple South Dakota outlets](https://www.dakotanewsnow.com/2026/07/13/south-dakota-receives-15-million-settlement-with-gaming-site-roblox/).
- **Active, unsettled litigation:** At least nine additional states have pending suits against Roblox (some jointly naming Discord), including Indiana (see the [Indiana AG Roblox/Discord report](reports/privacy/enforcement-actions/indiana-roblox-discord-child-safety-dcsa-2026-05-07.md)), Arkansas (see the [Arkansas AG Roblox/Discord report](reports/privacy/enforcement-actions/arkansas-ag-roblox-discord-lawsuit-2026-06-22.md)), Kentucky, Louisiana, Nebraska, Tennessee, Texas, Iowa, and Florida.

## Action Items

- **Platforms serving minors in South Dakota:** Review chat, age-verification, and parental-control architecture against the South Dakota AVC's requirements — facial age estimation or government ID verification, "trusted friend" chat gating, and unencrypted minor communications for law enforcement access — which now mirror the emerging multistate baseline established by Alabama, Nevada, and West Virginia.
- **Compliance teams tracking the Roblox multistate settlement wave:** Add South Dakota to the settlement tracker; note the most-favored-nation clause, which means concessions Roblox grants to any future settling state may retroactively benefit South Dakota (and vice versa for the earlier-settling states).
- **Verify primary sources before citing this development externally:** Given the WebFetch limitation in this research session, confirm the exact figures (especially the $5.4M vs. $54M contingent-penalty discrepancy and the precise SDCL 37-24 citation) against the official AVC PDF at `https://atg.sd.gov/docs/Roblox%20SD%20-%20AVC.pdf` before relying on this report for court filings or client advice requiring exact figures.
- **Monitor for the next settling state:** With five settlements and at least nine active suits pending, additional Roblox settlements are likely in the near term; watch for MFN-clause-driven convergence toward a single national compliance standard.
