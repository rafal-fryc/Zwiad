# Iowa AG Sues Temu for Data Harvesting and Deceptive Practices Under Iowa Consumer Fraud Act

**Jurisdiction:** Iowa | **Category:** Privacy | **Date:** July 1, 2026

## Summary [HIGH confidence] [VERIFIED]

Iowa Attorney General Brenna Bird filed an 86-page lawsuit on [July 1, 2026, in Polk County District Court](https://www.iowaattorneygeneral.gov/newsroom/attorney-general-brenna-bird-announces-lawsuit-against-global-e-commerce-marketplace-temu-for-violat) against e-commerce platform Temu (operated by Whaleco Inc., a subsidiary of China-based [PDD Holdings](https://www.courthousenews.com/iowa-accuses-temu-of-collecting-private-consumer-data/)), alleging widespread violations of the [Iowa Consumer Fraud Act, Iowa Code § 714.16](https://www.legis.iowa.gov/docs/code/714.16.pdf). <!-- verified --> The complaint centers on Temu's covert collection of sensitive consumer data — including precise geolocation, other-app inventory data, device identifiers, and Wi-Fi network information — allegedly harvested without adequate disclosure and potentially accessible to the Chinese Communist Party, alongside deceptive pricing, sign-up scams, and sales of counterfeit merchandise bearing University of Iowa trademarks. <!-- verified --> Iowa seeks injunctive relief, civil penalties of up to $40,000 per violation, restitution, and disgorgement, joining a growing multistate enforcement campaign against Temu. <!-- verified -->

## Key Facts [HIGH confidence] [VERIFIED]

- Bird's office filed the [86-page petition](https://qconline.com/news/state-regional/government-politics/article_f0bd9d64-7dd7-56ff-b4b4-3e03bcbbdcd3.html) on **July 1, 2026**, in **Polk County District Court**, naming Whaleco Inc. and its parent, PDD Holdings (formerly Pinduoduo), as defendants. ([Iowa AG press release](https://www.iowaattorneygeneral.gov/newsroom/attorney-general-brenna-bird-announces-lawsuit-against-global-e-commerce-marketplace-temu-for-violat)) <!-- verified -->
- The complaint alleges Temu **covertly collected** precise location data, information about other apps installed on users' devices, account information, device identifiers, and Wi-Fi network data, without meaningful consumer disclosure — data the AG's office says "could be accessed by the Chinese Communist Party." ([Iowa AG press release](https://www.iowaattorneygeneral.gov/newsroom/attorney-general-brenna-bird-announces-lawsuit-against-global-e-commerce-marketplace-temu-for-violat); [Courthouse News](https://www.courthousenews.com/iowa-accuses-temu-of-collecting-private-consumer-data/)) <!-- verified -->
- Iowa is asking the court to impose **civil penalties of up to $40,000 per violation** under the Iowa Consumer Fraud Act. ([LegalTech Digest](https://www.legaltechdigest.com/news/iowa-sues-temu-july-1-over-data-privacy-china-surveillance-risk)) <!-- verified: independently confirmed via Iowa Code § 714.16 text and news search -->
- Beyond data practices, the complaint alleges **deceptive pricing** (false reference pricing and misleading discounts), **sign-up and referral scams** that promised rewards never delivered, undelivered or mis-shipped orders, and sale of **counterfeit merchandise** using University of Iowa Hawkeyes trademarks, including fake IOWA Wave apparel whose legitimate sales benefit pediatric cancer research at University of Iowa Health Care Stead Family Children's Hospital. ([Iowa AG press release](https://www.iowaattorneygeneral.gov/newsroom/attorney-general-brenna-bird-announces-lawsuit-against-global-e-commerce-marketplace-temu-for-violat); [Muscatine Journal, "7 things to know"](https://muscatinejournal.com/news/state-regional/government-politics/article_a457c55a-02eb-506e-aa27-83b91a8f0b7e.html)) <!-- verified -->
- The state seeks **injunctive relief, civil penalties, restitution, and disgorgement**. ([Muscatine Journal](https://muscatinejournal.com/news/state-regional/government-politics/article_a457c55a-02eb-506e-aa27-83b91a8f0b7e.html)) <!-- verified -->
- Iowa's suit follows a wave of similar Temu enforcement actions by **Arkansas** (2024), **Nebraska** (June 2025), **Kentucky** (July 2025), **Arizona** (December 2025), **Texas** (February 2026), and **Oklahoma** (May 2026), all alleging illegal data harvesting and CCP-linked data sharing. ([Iowa AG press release](https://www.iowaattorneygeneral.gov/newsroom/attorney-general-brenna-bird-announces-lawsuit-against-global-e-commerce-marketplace-temu-for-violat); see also [Oklahoma AG Temu suit report](oklahoma-temu-ocpa-data-sharing-2026-05-18.md)) <!-- verified: independent search confirms Arkansas filed 6/25/2024, Nebraska 6/11/2025, Kentucky 7/17/2025, Arizona 12/2/2025, Texas 2/20/2026; see minor sourcing note in reviewer feedback -->

## Legal Framework [MEDIUM confidence] [VERIFIED]

The suit is brought under **Iowa Code § 714.16 (Consumer frauds)**, part of Iowa's Theft, Fraud, and Related Offenses chapter. Section 714.16 authorizes the Iowa Attorney General to bring civil actions in district court against persons engaging in "unfair practice[s], deception, fraud, false pretense, false promise, or misrepresentation" in connection with the sale of merchandise, and to seek [temporary restraining orders, injunctions, civil penalties, and restitution](https://www.legis.iowa.gov/docs/code/714.16.pdf). <!-- verified --> Iowa does not yet have a comprehensive consumer data privacy statute with a private right of action or AG rulemaking authority specific to data practices; as in the parallel Oklahoma, Texas, and Arkansas Temu suits, Bird's office is relying on the state's general consumer fraud statute to reach undisclosed data collection and cross-border data transfer practices, framing them as deceptive omissions rather than proceeding under a dedicated privacy law. This is the same enforcement pattern documented in the [Oklahoma AG's Temu suit](oklahoma-temu-ocpa-data-sharing-2026-05-18.md), which relied on the Oklahoma Consumer Protection Act rather than Oklahoma's not-yet-effective Consumer Data Privacy Act. <!-- verified -->

Official text could not be independently re-verified against the filed petition itself (the complaint document was not located at a public court-records URL during this research); the summary above relies on the AG's official press release and multiple news outlets that reviewed the filed petition. The statute citation (Iowa Code § 714.16) is confirmed against the [official Iowa Legislature code text](https://www.legis.iowa.gov/docs/code/714.16.pdf). <!-- verified -->

## Action Items

- **E-commerce and marketplace platforms with China-based ownership or data infrastructure:** Audit mobile app permissions and data flows against Iowa Code § 714.16 and equivalent consumer-fraud statutes in other states. Undisclosed collection of geolocation, other-app data, and device identifiers is now a recurring AG enforcement theory across at least seven states.
- **Retailers using algorithmic or reference pricing:** Review discount and "was/now" pricing disclosures for accuracy; false reference pricing is a specific allegation in this and the parallel Oklahoma complaint.
- **Brand owners and licensors:** Monitor third-party marketplaces for counterfeit use of institutional trademarks (e.g., university athletics branding); Iowa's complaint highlights IP enforcement as a companion theory to data-privacy claims.
- **Track docket developments** in Polk County District Court and monitor for coordination with the parallel Oklahoma, Texas, Arkansas, Nebraska, Kentucky, and Arizona actions, which could presage a multistate settlement.
