# New Jersey Enacts Nation's Costliest Data Broker Law (A5328), Effective Immediately

**Jurisdiction:** New Jersey | **Category:** Privacy | **Date:** 2026-07-02

## Summary [MEDIUM confidence] [VERIFIED]

On June 30, 2026, Governor Mikie Sherrill signed [A5328](https://pub.njleg.state.nj.us/Bills/2026/A5500/5328_I1.HTM) into law, making New Jersey the seventh U.S. state -- and the second in 2026, after Connecticut -- to enact a standalone data broker law, per [IAPP's reporting](https://iapp.org/news/a/independence-day-surprise-new-jersey-s-costly-new-data-broker-law). <!-- verified: independently corroborated via WebSearch across IAPP, Recording Law, and other secondary sources --> The law is unusually broad: in addition to regulating traditional "data brokers," it creates parallel registration and fee obligations for "data collectors" -- businesses with a direct consumer relationship (e.g., retailers, publishers) that sell personal data they collected to a data broker, a category not found in any of the four prior state data broker laws (California, Oregon, Texas, and Vermont), according to [Wiley's client alert](https://www.wiley.law/alert-New-Jersey-Adopts-Sweeping-New-Data-Broker-Law-Effective-Immediately). <!-- verified --> Most provisions, including a nationwide-first ban on the sale of "sensitive data" and steep civil penalties, took effect immediately upon signing; only the public registry requirement is delayed until March 27, 2027. <!-- verified --> On July 10-11, 2026, the New Jersey Division of Consumer Affairs (DCA) further announced it will not enforce the registration/fee requirements until June 2027, following business pushback over cost and scope confusion, per [Bloomberg Law](https://news.bloomberglaw.com/privacy-and-data-security/new-jersey-delays-enforcing-highest-in-nation-data-broker-fees). <!-- disputed: independent search corroborates the June 2027 enforcement delay and a DCA alert, but sources vary on the exact announcement date (some indicate July 1, 2026 for an initial alert vs. July 10, 2026 for a clarifying alert); exact date could not be pinned to a single primary source in this review -->

## Key Facts [MEDIUM confidence] [VERIFIED]

- Governor Sherrill signed A5328 on June 30, 2026; the bulk of the law took effect that same day, making New Jersey the seventh state with a data broker registration law ([IAPP](https://iapp.org/news/a/independence-day-surprise-new-jersey-s-costly-new-data-broker-law); [dataguidance](https://www.dataguidance.com/news/new-jersey-law-enacted-requiring-data-broker)). <!-- verified -->
- The law amends the New Jersey Data Privacy Act (NJDPA) framework to add a new "data collector" category: an entity that "knowingly (1) collects the personal data of a consumer with whom the data collector has a direct relationship; and (2) sells or licenses such personal data to a data broker" -- exposing retailers, content publishers, and other consumer-facing businesses to the same registration regime as traditional data brokers ([Wiley](https://www.wiley.law/alert-New-Jersey-Adopts-Sweeping-New-Data-Broker-Law-Effective-Immediately); [Future of Privacy Forum](https://fpf.org/blog/data-brokers-beyond-navigating-new-jerseys-data-broker-data-collector-registration-law/)). <!-- verified -->
- Annual registration fees are tiered by the number of New Jersey consumers whose data is handled, ranging from $5,000 (100,000 or fewer consumers) up to $1.5 million (more than 4.5 million consumers) -- exceeding California's previous high of roughly $6,000 and making New Jersey's fee schedule the most expensive in the country ([Bloomberg Law](https://news.bloomberglaw.com/privacy-and-data-security/new-jersey-delays-enforcing-highest-in-nation-data-broker-fees); [Troutman/Regulatory Oversight](https://www.regulatoryoversight.com/2026/07/new-jersey-enacts-the-nations-costliest-data-broker-law-yet/)). <!-- verified: fee floor/ceiling and CA comparison confirmed via independent search -->
- The law bans the sale of "sensitive data" by any individual or legal entity -- not just entities otherwise covered by the NJDPA -- covering data revealing racial/ethnic origin, religious beliefs, mental or physical health conditions, financial account access credentials, sex life or sexual orientation, citizenship/immigration status, transgender or non-binary status, unique-identifying genetic or biometric data, data of a known child, and precise geolocation ([McDermott Will & Emery](https://www.mcdermottlaw.com/insights/new-jersey-bans-the-sale-of-sensitive-data/)). <!-- verified -->
- Civil penalties are severe: up to $2,500 per day for failure to register, pay fees, or timely update registration information, and $50,000 per record for selling, offering to sell, or licensing sensitive data ([McDermott Will & Emery](https://www.mcdermottlaw.com/insights/new-jersey-bans-the-sale-of-sensitive-data/); [Wiley](https://www.wiley.law/alert-New-Jersey-Adopts-Sweeping-New-Data-Broker-Law-Effective-Immediately)). <!-- verified -->
- The requirement that the DCA build and maintain a public data broker/data collector registry is delayed until March 27, 2027 (270 days after enactment) -- this was written into the statute at signing, distinct from the later enforcement delay described below ([Wiley](https://www.wiley.law/alert-New-Jersey-Adopts-Sweeping-New-Data-Broker-Law-Effective-Immediately)). <!-- verified -->
- On July 10-11, 2026, the DCA announced via an online alert that it will not enforce the registration and annual fee requirements until June 2027, citing the need to issue further guidance on the law's scope; the sensitive-data sale ban and its associated $50,000-per-record penalty remain in force immediately and are unaffected by this delay ([Bloomberg Law](https://news.bloomberglaw.com/privacy-and-data-security/new-jersey-delays-enforcing-highest-in-nation-data-broker-fees); [Law360](https://www.law360.com/articles/2500348/nj-delays-registry-aspect-of-newly-enacted-data-broker-law)). <!-- disputed: June 2027 delay and sensitive-data-ban carve-out independently confirmed, but the precise "July 10-11" announcement date is not independently pinned down (secondary sources reference both a July 1 and a July 10 DCA alert) -->

## Action Items

- Businesses that sell or license New Jersey consumers' personal data -- including retailers and publishers with no prior data-broker registration obligation -- should immediately assess whether they meet the new "data collector" definition, given its unprecedented scope.
- Confirm whether any sensitive data (as broadly defined above) is sold, licensed, or offered for sale to third parties; this prohibition is in force now regardless of the DCA's enforcement delay on registration, and carries a $50,000-per-record penalty.
- Monitor the DCA's website for forthcoming guidance on scope and registration mechanics before the June 2027 enforcement date for registration/fees.
- Track the March 27, 2027 public registry launch date, since public listing may create reputational and competitive-intelligence exposure independent of the fee obligation.
- Confirm the exact statutory text and fee-tier thresholds against the official bill (link below) before finalizing any compliance budget, since this report's fee figures rely on secondary reporting.

## Update 2026-07-17

**Change:** Enacted June 30, 2026; DCA enforcement of registration/fee requirements delayed to June 2027 per July 10-11, 2026 announcement → Enforcement suspended pending a legislative amendment to fix drafting errors (notably the lack of a political-campaign/voter-data exemption); registry launch specifically targeted for spring 2027 with an April 1 - June 30, 2027 first registration window
**Source:** http://info.iapp.org/MTM4LUVaTS0wNDIAAAGi-2ecPgYwysh3X3NYP74b6AagYW1v6dMYYHqBm9JgECi4XoJfOmleqoI5nPJott790_1X4RI=
**Summary:** A senior Sherrill administration official confirmed on July 13, 2026 that New Jersey will not enforce the data broker/data collector registration requirements of A5328 until the Legislature amends the law to fix drafting errors, chief among them the absence of any exemption for political campaign and party voter-file/voter-targeting data -- a gap that reportedly threatened to force vendors such as NGP VAN out of the state, per the New Jersey Globe. The law was enacted just two days after introduction during budget negotiations, which critics cite as the cause of the oversight. Separately, the state's data broker/data collector registry -- whose statutory effective date is March 27, 2027 -- is now expected to formally launch in spring 2027, with the first registration window running April 1 through June 30, 2027, according to ZwillGen's client alert and further corroborated by National Law Review's coverage of AG clarifications. The sensitive-data sale ban and its $50,000-per-record penalty remain unaffected and in force.
