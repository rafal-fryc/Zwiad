(17)  'Exposure' means the degree to which a trusting party has made themselves vulnerable to harm or loss due to entrusting a covered entity with the trusting party's data and mediated experiences. A covered entity shall assess a trusting party's exposure by considering the nature and length of the relationship between the parties; the nature of the data collected, processed, or transferred, including whether any of the data is sensitive covered data; and the nature of the choices and signals mediated or controlled by the covered entity.
