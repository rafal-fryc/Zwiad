Measure Page FAQ

Subscribing to a Measure

  • If you are logged in to your account, select "Subscribe" on the measure status page to receive email notifications when a hearing notice is filed for the measure.
  • If you have an account but are not logged in, selecting "Subscribe" will redirect you to the login screen. After you log in, the system will automatically subscribe you to hearing notice email notifications for this measure then return you to the measure status page.
  • If you do not have an account, please Register first. You must confirm your email address (using the link in the confirmation email) before you can log in and subscribe to hearing notice email notifications.
  • Once subscribed, email notifications will be sent to the email associated with your account.
  • Select Unsubscribe on the measure status page at any time to stop receiving email notifications for that measure.
  • You may manage all email notification subscriptions from your account’s hearing notifications page.

What are RSS feeds?

RSS feeds let you get updates when new information is published—for example, this measure’s status. You can use a feed reader or browser to follow feeds.

View RSS feed help page for more details.

How to Use the Search

Using Search Filters

Click in the search box to see available filters. You can filter your search by content type:

  • Measures - Search bills, resolutions, and other legislative measures
  • Hearings - Find upcoming and past committee hearings
  • HRS - Search Hawaii Revised Statutes (state laws)
  • Legislators - Find legislators by name or district
  • Committees - Search for committee information
  • Documents - Search PDF documents and reports

Selecting Filters:

  • Click a filter pill to select it (it will highlight when active)
  • Select multiple filters to search across different content types
  • Click "Enable all" to search all content types at once
  • If no filters are selected, the search will include all content types

Searching for Bills

To find a specific bill or measure, simply type the bill number in the search box (e.g., SB123, HB456, HCR45). The search will automatically detect bill numbers and take you directly to the measure status page.


Search Tips

  • Use the Measures filter to search bills, resolutions, and other legislative measures from the current session
  • Use the HRS filter to search Hawaii Revised Statutes (state laws). To find a specific section, use the "Browse HRS Sections" link on the HRS page
  • Use the Hearings filter to find upcoming and past committee hearings
  • Use the Legislators filter to find legislators by name or district
  • Use the Committees filter to search for committee information
  • Use the Documents filter to search PDF documents, committee reports, and other files

Note: You can combine multiple filters to search across different content types simultaneously. If you don't select any filters, the search will include all content types.
