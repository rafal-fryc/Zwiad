## SENATE BILL No. 372

By Committee on Judiciary

1-22

- AN   ACT   concerning   consumer   protection;   enacting   the   app   store accountability act; regulating app store and developer operations with respect   to   minors;   creating   requirements   for   age   verification   and parental   consent;   providing   for   enforcement   under   the   Kansas consumer   protection   act;   creating   a   private   cause   of   action   for violations of the act.

Be it enacted by the Legislature of the State of Kansas:

Section 1. The provisions of sections 1 through 9, and amendments thereto, shall be known and may be cited as the app store accountability act.

- Sec. 2. As used in sections 1 through 9, and amendments thereto:
- (a) "Account holder" means the individual who is associated with the mobile device.
- (b) "Age   category"   means   one   of   the   following   categories   of individuals based on age:
- (1) "Child" means an individual who is under 13 years of age;
- (2) "younger teenager" means an individual who is at least 13 years of age and less than 16 years of age;
- (3) "older teenager" means an individual who is at least 16 years of age and less than 18 years of age; and
- (4) "adult" means an individual who is at least 18 years of age.

(h) "Content description" means a description of the specific content elements or functions that informs an app's age rating.

(i) "Developer" means a person that owns or controls an app made available through an app store or a pre-installed application.

(j) "Minor" means an individual under 18 years of age unless the individual is married or legally emancipated.

(k) "Minor account" means an account with an app store provider that is established by an individual who is a minor.

(l) "Mobile device" means a phone or general-purpose tablet that:

- (1) Provides cellular or wireless connectivity;
- (2) is capable of connecting to the internet;
- (3) runs a mobile operating system; and
- (4) is capable of running apps through such mobile operating system.
5. (m) "Mobile operating system" means software that:
- (1) Manages mobile device hardware resources;
- (2) provides common services for mobile device programs;
- (3) controls memory allocation; and
- (4) provides interfaces for apps to access device functionality.

(n) "Parent" means, with respect to a minor, an individual who is reasonably believed to be a parent, legal guardian, individual with legal custody or any other individual who has  the  legal   authority  to   make decisions on behalf of the minor under applicable state law.

(o) "Parent account" means an account with an app store provider that:

(1) Is verified to be established by an individual who the app store provider has determined through the app store provider's age verification methods is at least 18 years of age, married or legally emancipated; and

- (2) may be affiliated with one or more minor accounts.

(p) "Parental consent disclosure" means the following information that an app store provider is required to clearly and conspicuously provide to the holder of a parent account prior to obtaining parental consent:

(1) The age rating of the app or in-app purchase, if any such age rating exists;

(2) the content description of the app or in-app purchase, if any such description exists;

(3) a description of the personal data:

- (A) Collected by the app from an account holder; and
- (B) shared by the app with a third party; and

(4) the methods implemented by the developer to protect the personal data if such personal data is collected.

(q) "Pre-installed applications" means any app, or portion thereof, that is present on a mobile device at the time of purchase, initial activation or   first   use   by   the   consumer,   including   browsers,   search   engines   and

Sec. 6. (a) A violation of section 3 or 4, and amendments thereto, is an unconscionable act and practice under the Kansas consumer protection act. For purposes of the remedies and  penalties provided by the Kansas consumer protection act:

(1) The person alleging a violation of section 3 or 4, and amendments thereto,   shall   be   deemed   a   consumer,   and   the   app   store   provider   or developer that violates such section shall be deemed the supplier; and

(2) proof of a consumer transaction shall not be required.
