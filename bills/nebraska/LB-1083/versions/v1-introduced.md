Section 1. Sections 1 to 9 of this act shall be known and may be cited as the Transparency in Artificial Intelligence Risk Management Act. Sec. 2. The Legislature finds and declares: (1)  Artificial  intelligence  has  great  potential  to  vastly  improve the  lives  of  all  Nebraskans  by  accelerating  our  economic  growth  and contributing to scientific research; (2) Experts within the artificial intelligence industry have warned that artificial intelligence could pose grave and catastrophic risks, and the  State  of  Nebraska  has  a  strong  interest  in  understanding  how  the largest  artificial  intelligence  companies  are  assessing  and  mitigating such risks; (3)  Recent  events  have  illuminated  the  potential  for  artificialintelligence  systems  to  pose  serious  risks  to  minors  interacting  with such systems; and (4) It is the intent of the Legislature to create more transparency around artificial intelligence, but collective safety will depend in part on large frontier developers taking due care proportional to the scale of the foreseeable risks. Sec. 3.
